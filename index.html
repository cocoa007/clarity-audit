Audits

CRITICAL BTC Prediction Market — Bitcoin-Anchored Prediction Market

phessophissy/btc-prediction-market (5 contracts: v1 + v2 + v3 + PMT token + SIP-010 trait)

Decentralized prediction market using Bitcoin block hashes for settlement. The claim-winnings function sends payouts back to the contract instead of the user due to tx-sender resolving to the contract address inside as-contract. The owner can drain all user deposits via withdraw-fees with no limit check. V2 and V3 are incomplete — they accept creation fees but have no betting, settlement, or claim functions. Emergency withdrawal has no timelock, enabling instant rug pulls. 11 findings total (2 critical, 2 high, 3 medium, 2 low, 2 informational).

CRITICAL StackHub — Multi-Contract DeFi Platform

AdekunleBamz/stackhub (4 contracts: NFT Marketplace, Token Launchpad, Staking Vault, Service Registry)

DeFi platform with four fee-generating contracts. The staking vault is custodial — funds go directly to the owner's wallet, not the contract, making it an architectural rug-pull vector. The token launchpad charges 5 STX to create "tokens" that are just map entries with no actual FT minting. Neither contract implements standard SIP traits. 11 findings total (2 critical, 2 high, 3 medium, 2 low, 2 informational).

CRITICAL FlashStack — Flash Loan Protocol

mattglory/Flashstack (13 contracts: core + sBTC token + 10 receivers + trait)

Atomic flash-minting protocol for sBTC against PoX-4 locked STX. The sBTC token has a deployer backdoor allowing unlimited minting outside the flash-loan mechanism. The repayment check uses balance deltas instead of supply invariants, enabling receivers to keep minted tokens. Fees are burned instead of collected — the protocol earns zero revenue. 12 findings total (2 critical, 3 high, 3 medium, 2 low, 2 informational).

CRITICAL SatGuard Protocol — Insurance Pool

Mosas2000/SatGuard-Protocol

Bitcoin-backed micro-insurance protocol with pool creation, contribution-weighted claim voting, and payouts. Withdrawal share calculation is an identity function (x*y/y=x), causing underflow for late withdrawers. Claimants can vote on their own claims. 10 findings total (2 critical, 2 high, 3 medium, 2 low, 1 informational).
