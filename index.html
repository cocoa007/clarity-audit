CRITICAL SentinelBridge — AI-Guided Bridge Security

Smithkeem/SentinelBridge

AI-guided cross-chain bridge security system with risk assessment, incident response, and dynamic limits. No actual token transfers occur — the bridge is pure bookkeeping. Transfer limit ratchets to zero permanently via integer division bug. 11 findings total (2 critical, 3 high, 3 medium).

CRITICAL DAO Governance — veToken Model (5 Contracts)

serayd61/stacks-dao-governance

Curve-inspired veToken DAO with governance token, proposal manager, timelock, treasury, and voting escrow. The voting escrow has an infinitely recursive to-int that aborts every lock attempt, the proposal manager uses hardcoded voting power with its threshold commented out, and the five contracts are not integrated with each other. 14 findings total (3 critical, 4 high, 4 medium).

February 21, 2026

CRITICAL StacksMint — Token Factory & Registry

StacksMint/StacksMintContract (registry + token template)

Two-contract token launchpad: a SIP-010 template for forking and a shared on-chain registry. The registry cannot verify ownership or metadata of registered tokens — anyone can register any contract principal with fake data, enabling impersonation and front-running attacks. 10 findings total (1 critical, 2 high, 3 medium).
